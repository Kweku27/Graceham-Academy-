WHO WE ARE

Every child has unique potential.

Graceham Academy is a Montessori-inspired school in Amasaman, Ghana, dedicated to providing holistic, inclusive, and quality education from Crèche through Junior High School.

We believe every child has unique potential. Our role is to nurture that potential by creating an environment where children can explore, think creatively, develop confidence, and grow into responsible leaders and lifelong learners.

Our approach combines child-centred learning with innovative teaching, experiential education, sustainability, and global exposure.

We are committed to making quality education accessible to every child while integrating environmental awareness and sustainable practices into the learning experience.

ACADEMICS

Strong foundations. Curious minds.

Our academic programmes are designed to develop strong foundations while encouraging curiosity, independence, creativity, and a lifelong love of learning.

01
PRE-SCHOOL

Ave Maria Preschool Department

Our Pre-School programme is child-centred and designed around the individual needs, abilities, and developmental stages of each child.

Inspired by Montessori principles, we build upon the natural characteristics of childhood— including curiosity, creativity, independence, self-discovery, and the desire to explore the world through the senses.

02
PRIMARY SCHOOL

Building Foundations for Life

Our Primary School follows Ghana's Standards-Based Curriculum, providing students with the knowledge, skills, attitudes, and values they need for successful progression into Junior High School and beyond.

We place particular emphasis on foundational skills, critical thinking, creativity, communication, problem-solving, and personal development.

03
JUNIOR HIGH SCHOOL

Preparing Students for What's Next

Our Junior High School is a three-year programme aligned with the Ghana Education Service (GES) Common Core Programme (CCP).

Students develop critical thinking, problem-solving, communication, and independent learning skills while preparing for the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE).
