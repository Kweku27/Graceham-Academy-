About Graceham Academy

At Graceham Academy, the child comes first.

We are a Montessori-inspired school in Amasaman, Ghana, dedicated to providing holistic, inclusive, and quality education from Crèche through Junior High School (Grade 9). We believe every child has unique potential and our role is to nurture that environment where each child can explore, think creatively, develop confidence, and grow into responsible leaders and lifelong learners.

Our Mission

To ensure that quality education is accessible, inclusive, and impactful for every child.

Our Strategy: Future-Ready Education for a Changing World

1. Exceptional Pedagogical Foundations

Using the Montessori approach for early childhood development, fostering curiosity, independence, and creativity, drawing on the Teaching at the Right Level (TaRL) methodology.

2. Experiential Learning & Greening

School gardens, food systems education, climate awareness, and practical sustainability initiatives helping students respond to local and global challenges.

3. Global Exposure

Developing foreign-language skills in French and German, strengthening digital literacy via WiDU Africa partnerships, and connecting young people globally.

Academics

Our academic departments: Pre-School, Primary School, and Junior High School.

Pre-School

Child-centred and designed around the individual needs, abilities, and developmental stages of each child, laying a joyful foundation.

Primary School

Follows Ghana's Standards-Based Curriculum, focusing on strong foundational skills, critical thinking, creativity, communication, and personal development.

Junior High School

Aligned with the GES Common Core Programme (CCP), preparing students for the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) and future transitions.
