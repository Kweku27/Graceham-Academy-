ABOUT US

About Graceham Academy

Graceham Academy is a Montessori-inspired school in Amasaman, Ghana, dedicated to providing holistic, inclusive, and quality education from Crèche through Junior High School (Grade 9).

We believe every child has unique potential and our role is to nurture that potential by creating an environment where each child can explore, think creatively, develop confidence, and grow into responsible leaders and lifelong learners.

The World's most powerful equalising tool against inequality in all forms is Education. Knowing this, we are committed to making quality education accessible to every child while greening our education.

At Graceham Academy, the child comes first.

OUR STRATEGY

Future-Ready Education for a Changing World

Future-Ready Adaptation is at the heart of our educational model.

We believe education should extend beyond the classroom wall. Through practical learning, community engagement, sustainability initiatives, and global partnerships, we seek to equip children and young people with the skills and mindset needed to navigate an increasingly interconnected world.

Our three strategic areas of focus:

01

Exceptional Pedagogical Foundations

We use the Montessori approach to support early childhood development, fostering curiosity, independence, creativity, self-discovery, and a love of learning.

For foundational literacy and numeracy, we also draw on the Teaching at the Right Level (TaRL) methodology. Our ambition is not only to strengthen learning outcomes for Graceham students but also to contribute to improved foundational learning within the wider community.

02

Experiential Learning & Greening Education

Through school gardens, food systems education, climate awareness, and practical sustainability initiatives, our students learn to understand and respond to the challenges affecting their communities and the wider world.

03

Global Exposure

We prepare our students to participate confidently in an increasingly global world. Our programmes provide opportunities to develop foreign-language skills in French and German, strengthen digital literacy through partnerships and initiatives such as WiDU Africa to develop our mini-ICT lab, and connect young people to global networks and opportunities.

Academics

Our academic departments span Pre-School, Primary School, and Junior High School.

01

Pre-School

Our Pre-School programme is child-centred and designed around the individual needs, abilities, and developmental stages of each child.

02

Primary School

Our Primary School follows Ghana's Standards-Based Curriculum, providing students with the knowledge, skills, attitudes, and values they need for successful progression into Junior High School and beyond.

We place particular emphasis on strong foundational skills, critical thinking, creativity, communication, problem-solving, and personal development.

03

Junior High School

Our Junior High School is a three-year programme aligned with the Ghana Education Service (GES) Common Core Programme (CCP).

The programme builds on students' foundational knowledge while developing their critical thinking, problem-solving, communication, and independent learning skills.

Students are prepared for the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) and their transition to the next stage of their education.
